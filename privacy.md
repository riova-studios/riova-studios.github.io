# Riova Poster – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

## About this app

Riova Poster is a private tool built and used only by Mario Bauernhofer (Riova) to publish his own videos to his own TikTok account @riovaclips. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

## What the app accesses

With the owner's explicit consent (TikTok Login Kit), the app uses the TikTok Content Posting API to upload and publish videos to the owner's own account and to read the basic settings of that account that are needed for posting (such as available privacy options).

## What data is collected and how it is used

The app only handles the owner's own TikTok account data and his own video files. It does not collect personal data of viewers, followers or any other third party. Access tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer and are never shared.

## Sharing and selling of data

No data is sold, shared with or transferred to third parties.

## Retention, revoking access and data deletion

The owner can revoke the app's access at any time in the TikTok app under Settings and privacy, Security and permissions, Apps and services. Deleting the stored token on the owner's computer also ends access. Because the app stores no data of other users, there is nothing further to delete. To request deletion of any data related to this app, email info.riovaofficial@gmail.com.
