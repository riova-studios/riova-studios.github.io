# Riova Studios

Riova Studios is the creator brand of Mario Bauernhofer (Riova). We publish videos and podcast episodes about discipline, building a life you control and personal growth on YouTube, Spotify and TikTok (@riovaclips).

## Riova Poster

Riova Poster is a private tool that publishes the owner's own videos to his own TikTok account @riovaclips. It uses TikTok Login Kit and the Content Posting API, works only with the owner's own account and videos, and is not offered to the public.

What it does:

- Lets the owner log in with TikTok and give consent.
- Uploads the owner's own short videos to his own account, either as a draft or as a post with the privacy settings he chooses.
- Does not read, store or process data of any other TikTok user.
